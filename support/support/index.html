FAQ

What information is shared with my family? Only the flights you choose to share, plus basic timing and station information. Crew identity details and company data are not shared.
How do I import a roster? Import a supported airline roster PDF (or text export) from the relevant screen in FlyFam.
What should I include when reporting a fault? A screenshot, the steps you took, device model, and iOS version help us investigate.
